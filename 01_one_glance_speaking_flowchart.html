Your mental shortcut

PAUSE → 3 WORDS → SPEAK

2 sec pause → 3 keywords → 3 short sentences
  • WHY-WHAT-NEXT introduces a situation or proposal.
  • P-R-N shares a recommendation or answers "why?"
  • Make your main point early, especially when senior officers are present.
  • If you forget a sentence, return to the next keyword. A pause is fine.
